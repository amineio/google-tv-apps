## Review Focus

- An old callback after cancel must never activate an account; pin this with `OAuthProtocolTest.canceledAttemptRejectsLateCallback`.
- A returning account mismatch must preserve the active registration; pin this with `OAuthProtocolTest.returningIdentityMismatchPreservesCredentials`.
- Two expiry checks must not consume a rotating refresh token twice; pin this with `SessionStoreTest.concurrentRefreshRunsOnce`.
- A disconnect after visible text must remain incomplete; pin this with `ResponsesClientTest.eofWithoutCompletionIsFailure`.
- Activity stop must suppress late recording, response, and TTS callbacks; pin this with `AskControllerTest.stopInvalidatesPendingCallbacks`.
